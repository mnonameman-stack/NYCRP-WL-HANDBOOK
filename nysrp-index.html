02.Core RP Rules

Fear RP

If someone pulls a gun on you, comply. You cannot run or draw your own weapon. Value your life as if it were real.

Fail RP

No unrealistic stunts. Car flipped? Call a tow truck. Damaged vehicle? Pull over and roleplay the damage — no driving into a mod shop mid-pursuit.

Cop Baiting

Do not speed past police stations or honk at officers to trigger a chase. It is unrealistic and not tolerated.

Prioritize Realism

You cannot rob a bank every 5 minutes. Develop a proper character storyline. No treating the server like a public lobby or team deathmatch.

Greenzones

No violence, robberies, or citizen arrests at spawn locations, hospitals, or government buildings where players are setting up their characters.

New Life Rule (NLR)

If you die, you forget everything from that scene. Spawn somewhere else, stay away for at least 15 minutes. No revenge allowed.

Powergaming

You cannot type that you magically escaped handcuffs. Every action must give the other player a fair chance to react.

Metagaming

You cannot use out-of-character information — Discord chats, stream sniping — to influence in-character decisions. Your character doesn't know names until shown an ID.

03.Civ & Weapons

  • No military-grade rifles as a walking civilian — it triggers an immediate tactical response from law enforcement.
  • Weapons must be properly concealed and require a valid RP reason to draw.
  • Self-defense requires real justification — not just "they insulted me or gave me a ticket".
  • Realistic driving physics — no civilian sedan through thick mud or over steep mountains.
  • Popped tires from spike strips = slow down immediately and stop. No driving on bare rims at 100mph.
  • Your character's wealth, lifestyle, and vehicle must match the backstory you wrote in your application.

04.Scenario Examples

Review the following situations to understand how the rules apply in practice. Every rule comes down to a simple question: would this happen in real life?

Fear RP
Correct

Two officers draw weapons and shout "Hands up!" — the suspect immediately raises their hands, complies with commands, and does not reach for their waistband.

Incorrect

"A suspect draws their pistol and attempts to shoot while two officers have rifles aimed directly at their head."

New Life Rule
Correct

After being killed in a gang shootout near the docks, you spawn at an uptown hospital, wait 15 minutes, and begin a completely separate roleplay — unrelated to the previous scene.

Incorrect

"You get shot during a robbery, respawn at the hospital, and immediately drive back to the same block to shoot the people who killed you."

Metagaming
Correct

You have no idea who the masked person in front of you is until they physically show you an ID card or introduce themselves out loud in-character.

Incorrect

"You read the floating name tag above a character's head and use that information to call them by name before they have identified themselves."

Fail RP — Vehicle Damage
Correct

After hitting a barrier at speed, you pull over, roleplay a broken engine, and call a mechanic via in-game phone. You wait for help before moving.

Incorrect

"Your car flips twice during a police chase. You land on the wheels and continue the chase at full speed as if nothing happened."

Powergaming
Correct

/me slowly attempts to work a hand free from the cuffs, giving the officer a chance to notice and respond before any escape is possible.

Incorrect

"/me instantly escapes from handcuffs using superhuman strength and sprints away at full speed with no chance for the officer to react."

Cop Baiting
Correct

You speed away from a pursuit because your character is fleeing a crime scene — there is a real, active roleplay reason for the interaction.

Incorrect

"You rev your engine repeatedly outside the police station and do donuts in the parking lot specifically to get officers to chase you for no story reason."
